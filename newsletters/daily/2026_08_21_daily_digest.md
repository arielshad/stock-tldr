<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Moderna (MRNA) +176.97% to $174.38 — first-ever Phase 3 mRNA cancer therapy win</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">MRNA nearly triples on Aug 19 as the Phase 3 INTerpath-001 trial of intismeran + KEYTRUDA cuts melanoma recurrence — the first Phase 3 win for any mRNA cancer therapy.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Moderna and Merck's Phase 3 INTerpath-001 trial of intismeran + KEYTRUDA met its primary recurrence-free survival endpoint in resected stage IIB–IV melanoma. MRNA closed +176.97% at $174.38, adding ~$44B in market cap in one session.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
INTerpath-001 enrolled 1,137 patients randomized 2:1 to intismeran (individualized mRNA neoantigen therapy) plus KEYTRUDA vs KEYTRUDA alone; both the primary RFS and key secondary distant metastasis-free survival endpoints were met.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
This validates the mRNA oncology platform after years of Phase 2 data, re-prices Moderna's cancer pipeline, and lifts mRNA peers — Merck plans to begin regulatory discussions within months for a large adjuvant-melanoma market.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Biotech, healthcare, and mRNA platform investors watching whether the neoantigen approach extends beyond melanoma to other tumor types.
</p>

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Treasury doubles long-bond buyback caps to ≥$4B/op; 30Y yield falls ~15bp to 5.18%, dollar tumbles</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Bessent's Treasury doubles long-bond buyback caps to at least $4B a pop, lopping ~15bp off the 30Y yield and sinking the dollar in a surprise mid-quarter intervention.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
The U.S. Treasury announced Aug 19 it will at least double long-bond buyback caps from $2B to ≥$4B per operation in the 10Y–20Y and 20Y–30Y sectors, effective Sept 9 through the Nov 4 refunding. The 30Y yield fell ~15bp to 5.18% on the news.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
The announcement came hours after the 30Y yield hit 5.337% — its highest since 2007 — driven by fiscal-deficit concerns and heavy AI-capex investment-grade issuance at the long end; DXY had its biggest one-day drop in three weeks and gold rose +3.7% to $4,493/oz, with silver above $65.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The step-up signals Treasury has drawn a de-facto line at the recent long-end selloff; released the same afternoon as the hawkish FOMC minutes, Bessent's intervention capped the yield reaction and let equities absorb both pieces of news without a sell-off.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Rates traders, duration allocators, and dollar/gold macro desks tracking Bessent's evolving market-support toolkit heading into Jackson Hole.

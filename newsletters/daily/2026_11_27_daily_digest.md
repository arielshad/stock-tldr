<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Eurozone leaders nominate Pablo Hernández de Cos as next ECB president</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Spain's Pablo Hernández de Cos nominated to replace Christine Lagarde as ECB president from Feb 7 2027.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
At the Special European Council in Brussels on Thursday Nov 26, eurozone leaders agreed to put forward Pablo Hernández de Cos, 55, for the ECB presidency. Christine Lagarde steps down early on February 7, 2027.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
De Cos has been BIS General Manager since July 2025 after serving as Governor of the Bank of Spain 2018–2024 and chairing the Basel Committee 2019–2024. He was the only remaining candidate after the Netherlands withdrew Klaas Knot and backed de Cos instead.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
STOXX 600 closed +1.8% at 636.98 on the continuity signal — Jefferies described him as a moderate "dove." Markets price ~90% odds of a 25bp ECB hike at the December meeting with euro-area CPI at 4.0%.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Macro traders, euro-rates desks, and European bank investors watching the Dec 11 ECB decision and the March 2027 de Cos debut.
</p>

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Tokyo November CPI +2.9% Y/Y — 16-month high, BoJ Dec hike odds ~80%</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Tokyo core CPI +2.9% Y/Y hits a 16-month high but misses 3.0% estimate — BoJ Dec hike odds ~80%.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Japan's Statistics Bureau published preliminary Tokyo CPI for November 2026 today. Headline rose +2.8% Y/Y and core (ex fresh food) accelerated to +2.9% — the fastest since July 2025 — but just missed the +3.0% consensus. Core-core (ex food &amp; energy) slowed to +2.5%.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
The pickup is driven almost entirely by energy: electricity +9.5% and city gas +13.7% Y/Y after subsidy roll-off. The core-core softness tells the BoJ underlying wage-driven inflation is easing even as the headline runs hot. The print arrived the day after BoJ board member Naoki Tamura called for faster hikes toward neutral.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
Tokyo CPI is a 3-week-ahead proxy for the national number and the last major inflation print before the BoJ Dec 17–18 meeting. Markets price ~80% odds of a 25bp hike to 1.50%. USD/JPY trades near 159.35 as the yen softens on the core-core miss.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Macro traders, JGB desks, and FX desks watching the Dec BoJ decision alongside a near-certain Dec FOMC hike.
</p>

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">USTR proposes Section 301 tariffs on 14 economies — China up to 20%, EU/Japan 15%, Vietnam up to 35%</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">USTR's Section 301 excess-capacity findings turn into proposed tariffs on 14 US trading partners — the post-SCOTUS tariff channel after IEEPA duties were struck down.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
On Nov 25, USTR Jamieson Greer determined that 14 economies' excess-capacity practices are actionable under Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974 and proposed country-specific tariff ceilings. No immediate effective date; a public-comment period opened with a Jan 5 2027 deadline.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Proposed caps (net of MFN and existing duties): China up to 20% (40% snap-back if the Jan 10 US-China truce lapses); EU, Japan, Korea, Switzerland, Taiwan at 15%; Vietnam up to 35%; India up to 25%. Carve-outs apply to Section 232 goods and consumer electronics outside China. Hearings begin Jan 25 2027.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
This is the administration's primary tariff channel now that SCOTUS struck down IEEPA duties in February. The Jan 5 comment deadline, Jan 10 US-China truce expiry, and Jan 25 hearings create a concentrated policy window. The EU immediately rejected the overcapacity finding and signaled possible retaliation.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Macro/trade desks, equal-weight S&amp;P traders, and import-heavy retailers and semiconductors importers with China-sourced supply chains.
</p>

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">HP Inc (HPQ) Q4 FY26 — revenue $16.0B beats, non-GAAP EPS $0.83 tops guide; HPQ +~11% on dividend raise</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">HP's FY26 print beats its own guide on PC strength and tariff refunds; stock pops ~11% on raised dividend and FY27 EPS floor of $3.15.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
HP Inc reported fiscal Q4 2026 results after the bell Nov 24. Q4 revenue of $16.0B rose 9.3% Y/Y and non-GAAP EPS of $0.83 exceeded the $0.69–$0.79 guide — both figures include a $0.15 benefit from tariff refunds. HP also raised its quarterly dividend and issued its first FY27 outlook.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>

</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">

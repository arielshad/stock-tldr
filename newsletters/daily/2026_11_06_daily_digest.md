<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Asian equity indexes opened Friday Nov 6 with the worst single-session cascade of 2026. Korea's Kospi hit a first-level circuit breaker minutes after the open, and Japan's Nikkei 225 crossed the 20%-off-peak threshold into bear-market territory.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Kospi triggered at 4,924.61 (-8.52%) at 9:03 a.m. Seoul — the 11th first-level halt of 2026 — breaking below 5,000 for the first time since February and sitting ~46% off the June 22 record. Samsung Electronics fell 9.98% intraday, SK Hynix fell 11.71%; Nikkei touched 56,745 (-7.1%), with SoftBank Group -11.6% and Rohm -20%.

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The Nikkei entering an official bear market signals the Asia-AI trade is unwinding, not just repricing. HBM/DRAM leverage runs through Samsung and SK Hynix; memory capex runs through Rohm and Resonac; and SoftBank cascades into the Arm and OpenAI-adjacent equity complex.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Macro traders, APAC equity desks, SOX / semi-cap longs.

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Bank of England hikes 25bp to 4.00% — first rise in 3+ years, CPI seen peaking ~5% in Q2 2027</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Bank of England delivered its first hike in more than three years, lifting Bank Rate 25bp to 4.00% on an 8-1 vote as it braces for CPI to peak near 5% in mid-2027.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
The Bank of England's MPC voted on Nov 5 2026 to raise Bank Rate from 3.75% to 4.00% — the first increase since the hiking cycle ended in 2023. Governor Bailey and seven others voted for +25bp; external member Catherine Mann dissented in favor of a larger +50bp to 4.25%.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>

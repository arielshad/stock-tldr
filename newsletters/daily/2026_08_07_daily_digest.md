<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Trade Desk's Q2 revenue growth slows to +3% Y/Y, EBITDA contracts, and Q3 guide implies a ~12% top-line decline — TTD craters 22% after hours.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
The Trade Desk reported Q2 CY2026 revenue of $715.1M, up only 3% from a year ago and $37M below the $752M consensus. Adjusted EPS of $0.34 missed the $0.40 estimate by 15%, and adj EBITDA of $241M fell from $271M Y/Y.
</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Q3 revenue guidance of "at least $650M" would mark the first ever Y/Y decline for the DSP leader — down ~12% from Q3 2025 — and lands ~$155M below the $805M Street estimate. New CFO Nate Olmstead said visibility is "somewhat more limited than it has been in recent history."
</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
TTD is the largest independent buy-side ad platform on the open internet, so a second consecutive guide-down signals share loss to walled gardens (Amazon DSP, Meta, Google) is accelerating. SSP names Magnite (+18%) and PubMatic diverged sharply AH as capital rotates within the ad-tech stack.
</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Ad-tech traders and digital-advertising desks watching open-internet vs. walled-garden share shifts.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">AppLovin's Q2 revenue and Q3 guide both fall short of Street estimates in what Piper called its first guide miss since IPO; stock -21%.</p>

</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>

</p>

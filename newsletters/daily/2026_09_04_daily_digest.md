<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Broadcom's AI-semi engine tripled to $16.7B in Q3, but Q4 guide of $34.8B undershot the buyside — stock -5% AH.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Broadcom reported record Q3 FY26 revenue of $29.6B (+86% Y/Y) after the Sept 2 close, with non-GAAP EPS $3.32 topping the $3.24 estimate. AI-semiconductor revenue hit $16.7B, surging 221% Y/Y and 54% Q/Q.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Non-GAAP gross margin held near 75% and operating margin ran 68%, generating $13.7B of free cash flow (+95% Y/Y). Q4 FY26 revenue is guided to $34.8B (+93% Y/Y) with AI semi at $21.7B (+236% Y/Y) — but the headline guide missed the $35.03B LSEG consensus.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The guide undershot the buyside and triggered a ~5% AH selloff from the $369.68 regular-session close. This resets the ceiling for the AI-capex trade heading into Q4 and raises scrutiny on custom-silicon peers MRVL and hyperscaler ASIC programs.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
AI-infrastructure bulls, semis pair-traders, and hyperscaler-capex desks tracking the XPU buildout at Google, Meta, and OpenAI-linked programs.
</p>

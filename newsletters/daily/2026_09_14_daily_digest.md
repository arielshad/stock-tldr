<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Reuters: Nvidia weighing $10B anchor cheque for Anthropic's ~$100B IPO at ~$2T valuation — would be largest listing in market history.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">

<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
An anchor commitment is a pre-marketing pledge by a lead investor to buy a set portion of an IPO — a signal that helps anchor the book and attract institutional demand. The reported $10B ticket would be roughly a tenth of the total raise. Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate had climbed above $65B by end-July 2026, from ~$9B at end-2025.

</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
The rally continues the post-earnings ramp from Amazon's Q2 2026 print (July 30), which showed AWS growing 36.7% Y/Y to $42.2B — the fastest AWS growth in 18 quarters. A 7% oil drop on Trump-cancels-Iran-strike de-escalation also lifted the broader tape (S&amp;P 500 +1.48% to 7,600.50).

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The milestone confirms the market has shifted from doubting Amazon's AI capex to rewarding it; AMZN now ranks as the third-largest US company by market cap behind NVDA and MSFT, cementing Big Tech's dominance in Q3 sector rotation.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">

Mega-cap PMs, index tilters, and AWS supply-chain names (chip and networking gear).

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">ISM Manufacturing PMI July jumps to 55.6 vs 54.0 est — highest since May 2022, 7th straight month of expansion.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
The ISM's July 2026 Manufacturing PMI came in at 55.6, beating the 54.0 consensus by 1.6 points and rising 2.3 pts from June — the highest reading since May 2022 and the seventh straight month of expansion. Released Monday morning August 3.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
New Orders climbed to 56.7 (+0.7 from June) and the Employment Index surged back into expansion at 52.8 from 49.7 (+3.1 pts). ISM said the reading implies +2.8% annualized real GDP growth; the overall economy has now expanded for 21 straight months on ISM's composite.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
A hot ISM this deep into a "higher for longer" Fed stance strengthens the hand of three FOMC hawk dissenters (Hammack, Kashkari, Logan) voting for hikes, fading rate-cut odds while cyclicals (XLI) got a lift into the S&amp;P's record close.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Rates traders, industrials PMs, and macro discretionary funds positioning ahead of Friday's July NFP.
</p>

</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">

Healthcare M&amp;A desks, pharma equity analysts, and event-driven funds tracking oncology consolidation risk.

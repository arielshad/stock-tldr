<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Jensen Huang steps down as Nvidia CEO; CFO Colette Kress named interim leader while board searches for permanent successor.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>

</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>

</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
Huang built Nvidia into the largest publicly traded company in the world and the single largest weight in US equity indices. The departure follows a week of disclosures including an admitted affair and House GOP demands for a closed-door briefing on Nov 18; Bernstein cut its price target to $200 from $400.
</p>

<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Every equity investor, AI-infrastructure allocators, and risk desks watching the world's largest chip company navigate a leadership transition.

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">October producer prices cool from September's shock but the 12-month rate climbs to 6.5% — highest since Nov 2022 — hardening the Fed's case for a December rate hike.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>

</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Final demand goods rose +0.5% M/M and +10.2% Y/Y — the largest goods annual gain since October 2022 — while services added +0.2% M/M for a fifth straight monthly increase. Food jumped +1.3% M/M while energy slipped -0.1% as crude fell 10.3%.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
Pipeline goods inflation at +10.2% Y/Y signals pass-through still has legs. CME FedWatch now shows ~89% probability of a 25bp hike at the December 8–9 FOMC (target would reach 4.00–4.25%); 10Y Treasuries hit ~5.14–5.17% intraday while the S&P 500 slid ~-0.5–0.6%.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Macro traders, rates desks, and Fed watchers positioning for the December 8–9 FOMC decision.

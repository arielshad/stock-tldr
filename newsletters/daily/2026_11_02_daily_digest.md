<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Binance $6.3B Security Breach — Largest Crypto Hack Ever</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Binance says a $6.3B hot-wallet exploit — the largest crypto theft ever — is 90% backed from company funds, with the FBI pointing to North Korea.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
On Thursday Oct 29 Binance disclosed a security incident that drained ~$6.3B of client token assets from its hot wallets. By Saturday the FBI publicly named TraderTraitor — a known North Korean state-sponsored unit — as the attacker.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Binance's post-mortem pointed to a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop giving access to a service container used to sign withdrawal batches. ~$3B of affected addresses were frozen on-chain: Tether froze $1.567B USDT, Circle $800M+ USDC, Ripple froze RLUSD, and Arbitrum's Security Council acted on ARB.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
Bitcoin fell to ~$77K (-4.9%) and BNB dropped ~8%, testing whether SAFU-plus-treasury can absorb a nine-figure loss without socializing it to users. By Saturday, Binance said 90% of stolen value was replaced and withdrawals were targeted to resume Nov 1.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Crypto desks, exchange risk managers, and stablecoin issuers watching on-chain freeze response benchmarks.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
At an online meeting on Nov 1, the seven OPEC+ core producers kept their December 2026 production quotas at the September required levels — the third consecutive monthly hold. Brent's expiring December contract settled at $117.64 on Oct 30, its highest since April.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Quotas held at Saudi 10.478 mbd, Russia 9.949 mbd, Iraq 4.431 mbd, with a further ~2 mbd cut layer still covering most members through end-2026. All 21 OPEC+ members meet Nov 29 to decide the 2027 production framework.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
Holding December flat removes near-term supply upside and keeps attention on the Nov 29 call as Gulf exports continue rebuilding from Iran-war disruption. Oil at Brent $117 is a live headwind for inflation fighters already watching sticky PCE.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Energy desks, macro traders, and refiners tracking the Iran-war disruption and 2027 quota baseline.

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">BOJ holds at 1.25% but hikes its inflation forecast and triples the ETF-unwind pace, with Takata voting for an immediate hike to 1.50%.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
The Bank of Japan's Policy Board voted 8–1 on Oct 30 to leave the short-term policy rate unchanged at 1.25%, with board member Hajime Takata dissenting for a 25bp hike to 1.50%. The Bank also announced it will accelerate ETF disposals from January 2027.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
The Outlook Report raised the FY2027 core CPI forecast to 2.6% (from 2.4%) and described underlying inflation as "at a level generally consistent with" the 2% target. ETF sales were raised to ~¥860B/yr (~$5.45B) from ~¥330B/yr, cutting the implied unwind period from ~112 years to ~43 years.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The combination of the hawkish forecast, the Takata dissent, and the sharply faster ETF-unwind puts a December hike firmly on the table — markets now price ~80% odds of a 25bp move at the Dec 17–18 meeting. USD/JPY traded below 157.50 after the statement.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
FX desks, JGB rates traders, and Japan equities investors watching the accelerated ETF disposal program.
</p>

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Fortinet (FTNT) -20% on Service-Revenue Cut and Growth Decel from +26% to +20% — Drags Cyber Sector</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Fortinet beat on EPS and billings and raised FY26 guides — but a service-revenue cut and growth slowdown from +26% to +20% dropped the stock ~20% in a single session.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Fortinet reported Q3 2026 revenue of $2.08B (+20% Y/Y) beating ~$2.06B consensus, with non-GAAP EPS $0.91 (+23%) beating $0.86 est and billings $2.34B (+29%). The stock fell ~26% AH Oct 28 and closed Oct 29 down 20.1%.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Product revenue surged 37% Y/Y on hardware refresh, but FY26 service revenue guidance was trimmed below consensus and overall revenue growth decelerated from +26% in Q2. FTNT had more than doubled YTD, leaving the setup priced for reacceleration that didn't come.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The sell-off pulled the whole cybersecurity cohort: Palo Alto, CrowdStrike, and Zscaler each -6-7% AH. Even with a $1.2B buyback upsize, growth deceleration is punished in an AI capex rotation that expects accelerating top-lines.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Cybersecurity sector traders and software analysts tracking the sector's post-AI-capex repricing.
</p>

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Berkshire Q2 13F — Alphabet leaps to third-largest holding at ~$37.8B; first net-buying quarter in 14</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Berkshire's Q2 2026 13F reveals an $37.8B Alphabet stake — third-largest — and the first net-buying quarter in 14, with $19.8B added net.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Berkshire Hathaway's Q2 2026 Form 13F-HR (accession 0001193125-26-352200) was accepted by the SEC at 4:05pm ET on Friday Aug 14, with tier-1 press dissecting it Saturday. The filing details holdings as of June 30, 2026 and shows a clear pivot from three-plus years of net selling to net buying, with Alphabet taking the marquee spot.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Berkshire held ~106M Alphabet shares worth ~$37.8B at quarter-end (up 83% from 57.8M shares), with ~60% of the 48.1M new shares coming via Alphabet's June $10B private placement. Net Q2 equity activity: $23.5B bought, $3.7B sold (+$19.8B net), ending 14 consecutive quarters of net selling.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
Alphabet is now Berkshire's third-largest holding at 9.4% of the portfolio, behind only Apple (22.0%) and American Express (17.1%) — the clearest signal yet of Greg Abel redeploying the cash pile at scale. Bank of America was trimmed and Constellation Brands was exited entirely.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Buffett-followers, mega-cap allocators, and AI-infrastructure watchers tracking Berkshire's growing exposure to the Google ecosystem.
</p>

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Broadcom (AVGO) -5.94% to $392.99 — BofA flags XPV AI-financing vehicle could stack ~$370B in debt by mid-2029</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Bank of America's credit-side analyst turns cautious on Broadcom's off-balance-sheet AI-financing vehicle even as equity fundamentals stay strong.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
BofA credit analyst Tom Curcuruto published a note Friday Aug 14 downgrading Broadcom's issuer and bond ratings, focused on the Apollo/Blackstone-led XPV Platform Broadcom co-established in June 2026. His model shows XPV could accumulate up to ~$370B of senior debt by mid-2029 at 20-gigawatt scale — including ~$150B of new issuance in 2027 alone.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
XPV is an off-balance-sheet vehicle that raises third-party capital to buy custom AI accelerators and leases them to frontier AI labs; the first deployment funds more than 1 GW of compute for Anthropic, with Broadcom backstopping lease payments capped at ~$29B on the initial tranche. BofA simultaneously raised its FY26 revenue and adjusted EBITDA estimates for AVGO.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
This is the first credible sell-side pushback quantifying the debt trajectory in AI-financing vehicles — Broadcom itself is not on the hook for $370B, but residual-value guarantees are what a downturn scenario would test. Watch credit spreads and read-through to Apollo/Blackstone private-credit vehicles.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
AI-infra credit desks, AVGO options traders, and private-credit allocators tracking off-balance-sheet AI capex financing.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">U.S. July retail sales fell 0.6% M/M — the biggest monthly drop in more than a year and well below the +0.1% consensus.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
The Census Bureau's advance monthly retail trade report for July 2026, released Friday morning. Headline sales fell 0.6% from June to a seasonally adjusted $763.6B, missing the Dow Jones consensus of +0.1%; Y/Y growth held at +5.0%.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Motor vehicles and parts dealers led the drop at -1.8%, followed by nonstore retailers (online) at -2.2% and gasoline stations at -0.9%. Gains came in clothing (+1.9%), health and personal care (+0.7%), and food services (+0.5%).
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The soft consumer print landed the same morning as a UMich sentiment miss, pulling the S&P off Thursday's record high (closed -0.17% at 7,785.76). Following this week's tame CPI and PPI, it sharpens the debate around September FOMC path amid the Powell/Warsh Fed-chair transition.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Macro traders and rate desks watching for the next data point before Jackson Hole (Aug 27–29) and the September FOMC (Sept 16–17).
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Two Wall Street desks upgrade Fox to a Buy on the same session — raising EBITDA math and shrinking the discount on the pending $22B Fox–Roku deal.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Karnovsky lifted FY27/FY28 adj EBITDA by 7%/9%; Cahall raised his FY27 EBITDA estimate to $4.12B (from $3.85B) and modeled ~$800M of World Cup ad revenue (from ~$600M+). At FOXA $68.71, the implied value of the Roku deal ($96 cash + 0.9693 FOXA per ROKU share) rises to ~$162.60, narrowing the deal discount to ~3.5%.

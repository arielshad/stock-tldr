<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">CXMT +466% on Shanghai STAR debut — Asia's biggest IPO of 2026 raises $8.6B, briefly makes China's most valuable listed company at ~$487B market cap</h2>
<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">China's top DRAM maker debuts +466% in mainland's biggest IPO since 2010 — briefly the most valuable Chinese listed name.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT), Hefei-based and China's only at-scale DRAM producer, listed on Shanghai's STAR Market on Monday July 27. The IPO priced at 8.66 yuan/share and raised 57.92B yuan (~$8.6B) — mainland China's largest since Agricultural Bank of China in 2010 — with shares opening above 49 yuan and vaulting ~466% intraday.
</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Retail demand oversubscribed the book 212×; only 6.73% of shares are freely tradable at listing. Post-debut market cap of ~3.3T yuan (~$487B) briefly overtook ICBC as China's most valuable listed company, though still smaller than Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron.
</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
CXMT's arrival at scale reshapes DRAM supply and price expectations just as HBM demand rips on AI training builds, crystallizing Beijing's push for memory self-sufficiency under US export controls — and landing the same session US memory names like SanDisk sell off on Chinese-competition fears.
</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Semis traders, memory desks, and China tech investors tracking HBM4 shipment cadence and US export-control developments.
</p>

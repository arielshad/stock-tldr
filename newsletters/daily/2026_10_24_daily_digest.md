<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Nasdaq -2.6% — AI-Capex Reckoning Collides With Iran Oil Spike</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Tech stocks got smacked as AI-spending fears collided with an oil spike on fresh Iran-war worries.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Thursday Oct 22 was a split-tape risk-off session. The Nasdaq fell 2.6% to 26,008, the S&P 500 shed 1.2% to 7,663, and the Dow finished roughly flat — driven by an AI-capex gut-check after Meta and Tesla's Wednesday prints and a spike in crude oil on fresh geopolitical risk.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Meta fell ~7% after a $9.89B teen-safety settlement charge and a $135–145B FY26 capex guide; Lam Research sank ~9%, Intel ~8%, CoreWeave ~10%. The NYT reported planned US-Israel strikes on Iran after Nov 3, sending Brent above $112 and WTI to a 5-month high near $100. VIX jumped 14% to 19.03.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
This is the first clear test of whether mega-cap AI prints will keep carrying the tape or trigger a vol regime change. Hyperscaler capex is accelerating, but the market is now charging a higher cost of capital for debt-funded build-outs — overlaid with a renewed Iran war premium on crude.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Tech and semi traders, macro desks, energy analysts watching the AAPL/MSFT/GOOGL/AMZN prints next week.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Bloomberg says SpaceX wants ≥80K US cell sites to launch a fourth nationwide carrier — tower REITs rip, carriers get hit.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Bloomberg (Thu Oct 22 after close) reported SpaceX is in talks with Crown Castle, American Tower, and Vertical Bridge to lease at least 80,000 US cell sites, pointing toward Elon Musk building a fourth nationwide wireless carrier alongside Starlink direct-to-cell.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
SpaceX already has FCC clearance for ~15,000 Starlink direct-to-smartphone satellites and recently closed an ~$8B acquisition of nationwide 800 MHz spectrum from Grain Management. Crown Castle CEO said on the Wednesday earnings call: "You can assume that we're talking to all parties."
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
If SpaceX leases 80K macro sites alongside Starlink D2C it re-introduces a credible fourth-carrier threat for the first time since Sprint/T-Mobile merged — repricing the spectrum, bundling, and FWA moats at AT&amp;T, Verizon, and T-Mobile. Bernstein called an 80K-site commitment "a point of no return."
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Telecom analysts, tower REIT holders (CCI +12%, AMT +7%), and Starlink watchers; status is unconfirmed Bloomberg scoop.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Record quarter and third FY26 guide hike, but a sudden swing to negative operating cash flow and a 3x capex ramp sent BE down 11% the next session.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Bloom Energy reported Q3 2026 after close on Oct 22. The solid-oxide fuel-cell maker — a key AI data-center power supplier via deals with Nebius, Brookfield, Oracle, and Equinix — posted record revenue of $1,235.8M (+138.1% Y/Y) and raised full-year guidance for the third time, including S&amp;P 500 index addition news.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Non-GAAP EPS was $0.94 and adj EBITDA $299.5M. FY26 revenue guide raised to $4.4–$4.6B (from $3.9–$4.2B). What spooked investors: operating cash flow swung to -$27.1M (from +$226M in Q2) as capex tripled to $153.5M, implying FCF of about -$181M for the quarter.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
BE is the cleanest listed proxy for AI data-center power delivery outside big-cap utilities. The cash-burn pivot is the first visible sign that fuel-cell AI power is a capex-heavy business — read-through to VST, CEG, TLN, PLUG, and FCEL.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
AI-infra and energy-transition desks tracking the data-center power build-out.
</p>

<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
American Express reported Q3 2026 before the open on Friday Oct 23. Both revenue ($20.1B, +9% Y/Y) and adjusted EPS ($4.68, +13% Y/Y) cleared Street estimates, and the company raised its full-year 2026 GAAP EPS guidance by about $1 at the midpoint. Shares traded modestly lower as investors digested one-time items.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>

FY26 GAAP EPS guide raised to $18.30–$18.60 (from $17.30–$17.90); revenue growth guide held at ~10%.

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Financials analysts and consumer-spending desks tracking the health of the premium card ecosystem.

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">AI-storage demand pushed WDC's quarter well above its own prior guide, and the Q2 outlook is an even bigger step-up.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>

</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
The beat is a margin story: non-GAAP gross margin hit 58.1% and operating margin 49.1% — record levels driven by tight supply for high-capacity nearline drives. For Q2 FY27, management guided revenue to $5.1B ±$100M (+66–72% Y/Y) and EPS to $5.80 ±$0.20, with GM stepping up to 60–61%. The board raised the quarterly dividend 17% to $0.175/share.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
WDC is one of two public HDD pure-plays left after the NAND split. The Q2 guidance step — from $4.38B to a $5.1B midpoint — is the biggest sequential guide ever issued, reinforcing the thesis that AI training and inference demand (~80% of hyperscaler data stays on HDDs) is durable. Read-through to Seagate (STX) and Marvell (MRVL).
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
AI-infra traders and semiconductor desks tracking the hyperscale storage cycle.

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">

Snap traded down 7–9% intraday after Pennsylvania AG filed a fresh parallel suit against Snapchat.

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">FDA approves Revolution Medicines (RVMD) Rasonque — first RAS(ON) inhibitor for previously-treated metastatic pancreatic cancer; Phase 3 mOS 13.2 vs 6.7 months; approved 6.5 months early; list price $39,800/month</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">First RAS(ON) inhibitor cleared for pancreatic cancer — Phase 3 nearly doubled median survival vs standard chemo.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
The FDA approved Revolution Medicines' Rasonque (daraxonrasib) on Aug 26 for adults with metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma who have received at least one prior systemic therapy. It is the first RAS(ON) multi-selective inhibitor approved in any indication and the first broad RAS-targeted therapy cleared for PDAC.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Approval rests on the randomized Phase 3 RASolute 302 trial (n=500) in previously-treated metastatic PDAC, where daraxonrasib produced a median overall survival of 13.2 months versus 6.7 months for standard chemotherapy. The FDA cleared the once-daily oral drug 6.5 months ahead of the PDUFA deadline.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
Pancreatic cancer has been one of oncology's hardest problems — RAS mutations sit in ~90% of PDAC tumors but have historically been un-druggable. A first-in-class RAS(ON) approval opens a pipeline read-across into CRC, NSCLC, and other RAS-driven cancers where RVMD, MRTX, and AMGN programs could benefit or compete.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Biotech desks, oncology specialists.
</p>

On Tuesday Aug 25, SpaceX and Louisiana Governor Jeff Landry announced 'Starbase Louisiana' — a $100 billion Starship launch complex on 125,000 acres of coastal Pecan Island in Vermilion Parish, billed as the largest launch site ever planned.

<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Five launch complexes with two pads each (10 launchpads total), propellant production at each complex, a deep-water shipping port, dedicated power generation, and on-site residential housing. Construction begins 2027, first launch targeted ~2029, designed for more than 30 Starship flights per day at full build-out.
</p>

Sympathy exposures include RKLB, ASTS, and Gulf-Coast industrial-gas suppliers.

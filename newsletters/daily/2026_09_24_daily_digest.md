<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">US-China Busan Agreement trade truce extended to Jan 10, 2027 — as Xi arrives in Washington</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">US-China Busan Agreement tariff truce pushed from Nov 10 to Jan 10 2027 as Xi lands in Washington.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on Fox News Wednesday Sept 23 that Washington and Beijing agreed to extend the 'Busan Agreement' — the year-old economic détente due to expire Nov 10 — through Jan 10 2027. The announcement came hours after Bessent held an unscheduled meeting with Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng in Washington.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
The Busan Agreement caps most US-China tariffs at current levels while both governments negotiate. Bessent said a larger economic package may be agreed by January or the deal may simply be extended again, adding that 'some deliverables have not been perfect on the Chinese side.'
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The extension removes the Nov 10 tariff cliff that macro desks had priced as a risk event. Rate-cut expectations, EM FX, industrial metals, and China-exposed US names (AAPL, TSLA, NVDA, DE, CAT) all had that November date on watch.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Macro traders, EM FX desks, trade-exposed multinationals, and anyone watching the Trump-Xi state-visit summit on Sept 24.
</p>

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">S&P Global US Flash PMI: Composite 58.4 — 5-year high. 10Y yield hits 5.07%</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">US private-sector activity hit its fastest pace since 2021 in September, with services and manufacturing both surging past consensus.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
S&P Global's flash PMI for September came in at 58.4 (composite), up from 56.0 in August — the strongest print in over five years. Services hit 58.7 vs a 56.0 estimate; manufacturing jumped to 57.0 from 53.2, well above the 53.6 consensus.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
PMI above 50 signals expansion. New orders rose the most in four-plus years; employment hit its highest since June 2022. Input costs surged at the steepest pace in four years on fuel and transport spikes, giving firms more pricing power on output.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The print reset near-term Fed pricing: the 10Y Treasury spiked to 5.07% intraday — the highest since 2007 — and October rate-hike odds jumped to ~64%. Stocks fell (S&P 500 -0.56%, Nasdaq -0.94%) as the yield surge weighed on rate-sensitive sectors.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Macro traders, rates desks, and Fed watchers tracking the path to October FOMC.
</p>

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Viking Therapeutics (VKTX) +27% — VK2735 holds 97% of weight loss on every-other-week dosing</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Viking's obesity drug held nearly all its weight loss on Q2W dosing, opening a shot at less-frequent GLP-1/GIP maintenance.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Viking Therapeutics released positive top-line data from a Phase-2 maintenance study of VK2735, its dual GLP-1/GIP agonist for obesity. After a 21-week weekly induction, responders were randomized to continued weekly, every-other-week, or monthly maintenance for 12 more weeks.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Weekly induction produced 16–19% placebo-adjusted weight loss at Week 21. Patients switched to every-other-week dosing retained up to 97% of that loss; monthly dosing retained up to 90% — versus only 61% on placebo. GI adverse events on maintenance were similar to placebo.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
Q2W and monthly maintenance is what payers and PBMs want to underwrite chronic obesity care. This is the first Phase-2 GLP-1/GIP read to hold most weight loss on less-frequent dosing — a competitive signal vs Lilly's tirzepatide and Novo's semaglutide.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Biotech / GLP-1 traders and small-cap holders watching the obesity-drug competitive landscape.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">IonQ shows real-time error correction running on a single CPU and a 2027 Nvidia research-center deployment for Superion 256.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>

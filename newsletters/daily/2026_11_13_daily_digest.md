<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Jensen Huang steps down as Nvidia CEO; CFO Colette Kress named interim leader while board searches for permanent successor.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Nvidia's board announced Thursday that Jensen Huang has resigned as president, CEO, and member of the board, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress takes over as interim CEO while retaining her CFO duties, and director Harvey C. Jones becomes the company's first-ever chair of the board.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
The board will consider both internal and external candidates for a permanent CEO with no timeline given. NVDA traded down as much as 14% intraday before recovering to close -4.34%, with the stock hovering around the $4 trillion market-cap mark. Q3 fiscal 2027 earnings remain scheduled for Nov 17.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
Huang built Nvidia from a graphics-card startup into the largest publicly traded company in the world. The transition follows a week of disclosures including an admitted affair with an executive under US intelligence scrutiny, a second affair reported by the NYT, and a House GOP briefing demand. Bernstein cut its price target to $200 from $400 ahead of the Nov 17 earnings print.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Every equity investor, AI-infrastructure allocators, risk desks — NVDA is the single largest weight in US equity indices.

Anthropic PBC, the Claude developer, filed an amended S-1/A with the SEC on Nov 10 setting the price range for its Nasdaq IPO under ticker ANT at $70–$78 per share for 825M Class A shares offered, with a concurrent $26B private placement at the IPO price. Pricing is expected Nov 18; first trading day Nov 19.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
At the top of the range, the base deal raises up to $64.4B in gross proceeds — the largest US IPO ever, ahead of Saudi Aramco and Alibaba's 2014 NYSE listing. On a fully diluted basis the valuation reaches up to $1.405T. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley lead a 24-bank underwriter syndicate.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
This is the first time a pure-play frontier-AI lab has set an IPO price range in a public filing. At $1T–$1.4T, Anthropic would list between Berkshire Hathaway and Google parent Alphabet by market cap on day one, anchoring private AI valuations at a critical moment in the AI-capex debate.
</p>

<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
IPO desks, AI-exposed allocators, and macro narrative traders watching the AI valuation anchor.

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">TSMC Board Approves Up to $1B in Anthropic + OpenAI Stakes — Plus $40.93B 2027 Capex; TSM +2.2%</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">TSMC's board green-lights up to $1B in Anthropic and OpenAI equity — days before Anthropic's IPO.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Taiwan Semiconductor's board authorized up to $500M to acquire Anthropic PBC common shares at Anthropic's IPO price and up to $500M for OpenAI Group PBC preferred stock. The same meeting also authorized up to $500M to establish a strategic semiconductor investment vehicle and ~$40.93B in 2027 capital appropriations.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
The Anthropic tranche is tied directly to the IPO price of $70–$78/share. The OpenAI tranche is a preferred-stock investment with no disclosed valuation or round terms. Both are caps, not committed purchases. TSM ADRs rose about 2.2% at midday on Wednesday following the disclosure.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The foundry that fabricates most frontier-AI silicon (Nvidia, AMD, Apple, Broadcom chips) is now taking direct equity stakes in the two largest model labs. It aligns TSM commercially with its biggest advanced-wafer customers, and the ~$40.93B 2027 capex authorization puts a number on TSM's next-year investment envelope.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Semiconductor investors and AI-infrastructure allocators watching the foundry-to-model-lab supply chain.
</p>

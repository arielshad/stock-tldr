<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">HARBOR misses primary endpoint — Novartis's flagship DM1 antibody-oligonucleotide conjugate can't beat placebo on hand-opening time.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Novartis's global Phase III HARBOR study of del-desiran (from its $12B Avidity buyout) did not meet its primary endpoint — video hand opening time (vHOT) — in myotonic dystrophy type 1 (DM1). The company cited "evidence of clinical activity" in secondary endpoints and said the program is under review for its next development path.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
HARBOR enrolled ~150 DM1 patients dosed every 8 weeks over 54 weeks; the PAK check passed with "generally consistent" safety data, pointing to a difficult efficacy bar on the vHOT endpoint rather than a safety signal. Novartis's other AOC assets (del-zota in DMD44, del-brax in FSHD) remain on track.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
This is the third Novartis R&amp;D setback in a week — after the pelacarsen Lp(a)HORIZON cardiovascular-outcomes miss on Sept 4 and the rap-cel CAR-T trial pause on Sept 1 after three patient deaths. Combined del-desiran + pelacarsen peak sales had been modeled near $5B.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Biotech traders and generalist pharma investors; negative read-across for Dyne, Sarepta (neuromuscular precedent), and Ionis (loses a partnered cardiovascular catalyst).
</p>

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">CoreWeave (CRWV) Q2 2026 — revenue $2.58B (+112% Y/Y), $104B backlog + $25B net-new Q3 commits; CRWV +~14% AH</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">CoreWeave posts +112% Y/Y revenue with a $104B backlog and $25B of net-new deals already in Q3 — GPU-cloud demand is not slowing.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
CoreWeave, the Nasdaq-listed AI cloud provider, reported Q2 2026 results on Aug 11 — revenue more than doubled Y/Y with backlog swelling to ~$104B, plus $25B of net-new customer commitments already booked in the first weeks of Q3.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Revenue was $2.575B (+112% Y/Y), adj EBITDA $1.51B at a 59% margin; GAAP net loss was $626M ($1.14/sh) as debt-funded capex continues to ramp. Active power grew ~500 MW to 1.5 GW; contracted power sits at ~3.7 GW.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The $104B backlog plus $25B added in three weeks means CRWV is essentially pre-sold through 2027; comp names DLR, IREN, APLD and NBIS trade in sympathy on capacity demand. Debt load and GAAP losses remain the counter-argument.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
AI-infrastructure and data-center desks.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">SMCI misses on Q4 revenue but doubles gross margin and guides FY27 revenue as much as $18B above Street expectations.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Super Micro Computer reported fiscal Q4 (ended June 30) after the close on Aug 11 — revenue came in light of Street expectations, but margins blew past management's own guide and FY27 revenue guidance was far above analyst models.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Q4 revenue was $11.1B vs the $11.55B consensus, but non-GAAP EPS was $1.70 (+315% Y/Y) vs $0.96 est on a 17.5% gross margin — nearly double the year-ago 9.5%. FY27 revenue was guided to $65–72B vs a $54.4B consensus.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The mix shift matters more than the headline miss — a 17.5% gross margin on $11B of revenue implies the record $60B of new orders SMCI flagged in July are landing on much better economics than the market had priced. The FY27 midpoint (~$68.5B) implies +75% Y/Y growth.
</p>

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Riot Platforms (RIOT) signs 20-year, $9.1B AI lease with Anthropic — 191 MW at Rockdale, TX; RIOT +~25% AH then +~18% premarket</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Bitcoin miner Riot pivots into AI landlord — $9.1B, 20-year, 191 MW lease with Anthropic at its Rockdale, TX campus.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Riot Platforms disclosed a 20-year data-center lease with "one of the world's leading frontier AI labs" for 191 MW of critical IT capacity at Rockdale, Texas, generating ~$9.1B in contracted revenue through June 2048 — Bloomberg identified the counterparty as Anthropic.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Riot delivers 96 MW by December 2027 and the full 191 MW by June 2028; estimated annual NOI is ~$365–$411M. Morgan Stanley provided a $573M interim financing facility for early construction, and two five-year extension options could take total contract value to ~$16.1B.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
This re-rates Riot from a Bitcoin miner to an AI landlord — long-dated lease cash flows now define the story more than bitcoin mining revenue, validating that frontier AI labs will buy capacity from any build-ready site. Combined with its existing AMD deal, Riot now has 241 MW of signed AI capacity.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
AI-infrastructure allocators, bitcoin miner equity investors, power/data-center desks.
</p>

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Nvidia (NVDA) teams with Apollo, Blackstone, BlackRock, Brookfield, Goldman and KKR on $500B AI infrastructure financing — NVDA -~3%, ~$130B market cap erased</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Six of Wall Street's biggest asset managers will build a &gt;$500B financing rail for AI infrastructure buyers of Nvidia hardware.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Nvidia and a coalition of Apollo, Blackstone, BlackRock (Global Infrastructure Partners), Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR will mobilize more than $500B of third-party capital for hyperscalers, frontier AI labs and enterprises to build data centers, power generation and buy Nvidia hardware.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
The consortium extends prior AI-financing programs (e.g., Apollo/Blackstone's June $35B Broadcom partnership) into a Nvidia-anchored rail across chips, energy and real assets; Jensen Huang told CNBC he approached only these six firms and none turned him down.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
It formalizes private-credit/infra funds as the plumbing of AI capex — letting buyers finance GPUs and data-center power off balance sheet — though several analysts have flagged the structure is "reminiscent of 1999." NVDA shed ~$130B of market cap as the market read the venture as a demand hedge more than a demand pull.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
AI-infrastructure allocators, private-credit desks, mega-cap semi investors.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">On Holding cracks after wholesale collapses to +4.8% and management pulls its ≥23% FY sales-growth floor.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
On Holding, the Swiss premium performance-shoe maker, reported Q2 2026 on Aug 11 — sales missed for the first time in six quarters as wholesale decelerated sharply and management cut the full-year growth floor.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>

</p>

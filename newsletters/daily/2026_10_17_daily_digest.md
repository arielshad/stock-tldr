<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">AI-debt fear tanks tech Fri Oct 16 — Nasdaq -2.15%, CoreWeave CDS hits ~1,000 bps</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">AI-debt fears hit tech Friday — Nasdaq -2.15%, CoreWeave bonds and CDS blow out to distress levels.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
A broad risk-off session for US tech on Fri Oct 16. The Nasdaq closed down 2.15% at 26,140.03, the S&P 500 down 1.09% at 7,659.44. The trigger was renewed worry that debt-financed AI infrastructure firms — led by CoreWeave — may struggle to repay as borrowing costs stay sticky after Wednesday's hot CPI and Thursday's hot PPI.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
CoreWeave shares fell ~12% on the day and ~25% over five sessions. Reuters cited the 5-year CDS near 1,000 bps (a record) and the April 2031 bonds yielding above 15% — distress territory. Spill-over hit chips: the PHLX Semi Index fell 2.5% for its eighth straight decline, with Micron, AMD, and Intel each down 5–6%.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The Nasdaq is on track for its worst week since June, with weekly losses north of 4%. The episode re-prices tail risk on the entire neocloud / AI-capex-funded-by-debt trade — from CRWV through NVDA customers to the full chip supply chain.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Equity macro traders, credit desks, and AI infrastructure watchers who need to size the debt-funded capex overhang.
</p>

An investor presentation cites voids (air pockets) in the carbon-composite forward dome of Neutron's first-stage propellant tank.

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">

</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
RKLB fell ~7.85% intraday to $59.18 then another -9.94% AH to $53.30 — a ~16% two-day drawdown that leaves the stock ~65% below its 52-week high of $151. This is the fourth Neutron slip and extends SpaceX's effective monopoly on US medium-lift reusable launch for another year.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Space-sector investors and defense & launch desks tracking the commercial launch market and SpaceX competition.

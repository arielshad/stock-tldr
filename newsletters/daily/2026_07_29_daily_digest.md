<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>

Post-debut market cap hit ~3.3 trillion yuan (~$487B).
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
The IPO priced at 8.66 yuan/share, retail books were 212× oversubscribed, and only ~6.73% of shares are freely tradable at listing; Q1 2026 revenue jumped 719% Y/Y to 50.8B yuan on the global AI-driven DRAM/HBM shortage.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
CXMT's listing reshapes DRAM supply and price expectations just as HBM demand rips on AI training builds, crystallizing Beijing's memory self-sufficiency push under US export controls — and it hit US names like SanDisk (SNDK) hard on the same session on competition fears.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">

Semis traders, memory desks, and China tech investors tracking the HBM4 and US export-control cadence.

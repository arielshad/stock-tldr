<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">U.S. July Jobs: NFP -23K — First Monthly Loss Since 2020; S&P 500 to Record as Sept Hike Odds Crater</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">BLS July jobs prints the first negative payrolls month since 2020, with combined May+June revisions of -103K — S&P sets a fresh record and the market yanks September Fed-hike odds sharply lower.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
The BLS July 2026 Employment Situation showed nonfarm payrolls declined by 23,000 versus the +80K consensus — the first monthly loss since December 2020. Prior two months lost a combined 103K in revisions.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
May revised to +63K (from +129K), June to +20K (from +57K); losses led by local government education (-50K) and leisure &amp; hospitality (-40K). The 10Y yield fell ~6bp to 4.62% and the S&P 500 closed at a record 7,757.64 (+0.62%).
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
This is the cleanest labor-side data point yet against Chair Warsh's hawkish tilt; a negative payrolls print with 103K in downward revisions stresses the labor-market pillar of the hike case. Rate-futures markets cut the probability of a September Fed hike to 43.9% from 57%.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Macro traders, front-end rates desks, and Fed-watchers tracking the Warsh-led FOMC's next move ahead of the August 13 CPI print.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Greg Abel's third full quarter as CEO: biggest buyback of 2026 ($9.6B) but cash pile keeps climbing to a record $377B as insurance underwriting plunges 43%.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>

Q3 buybacks jumped to $9.6B — the largest quarter of 2026 and more than double Q2's $4.5B — bringing the nine-month total to $14.4B. Despite the buyback step-up, cash + Treasuries still hit a record $377.4B at Sept 30.

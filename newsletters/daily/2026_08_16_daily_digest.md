<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">AVGO -5.94% as BofA flags XPV vehicle could stack $370B in debt by 2029</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Bank of America's credit-side analyst turns cautious on Broadcom's off-balance-sheet AI-financing vehicle even as equity fundamentals stay strong.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
BofA credit analyst Tom Curcuruto downgraded Broadcom's issuer and bond ratings on Aug 14, focused on the AI XPV Platform co-established with Apollo and Blackstone in June 2026. His model shows the vehicle could accumulate up to ~$370B of senior debt by mid-2029 at 20 GW scale. Shares fell 5.94% to $392.99, though BofA simultaneously raised its FY26 revenue and EBITDA estimates.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
XPV is an off-balance-sheet vehicle that raises third-party capital to buy custom AI accelerators and leases them to frontier AI labs. Apollo and Blackstone led a $35B initial financing in June 2026; the first deployment funds more than 1 GW of compute for Anthropic. Broadcom backstops lease payments for five years, capped at ~$29B — the $370B is the vehicle's total projected senior-debt stack at 20 GW scale, not Broadcom's liability.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
This is the first credible sell-side pushback quantifying the debt trajectory of AI-financing vehicles that let hyperscalers finance GPU capex off balance sheet. Watch Broadcom credit spreads and read-through to Apollo/Blackstone private-credit vehicles; NVDA Aug 26 print is the next major catalyst.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
AI-infra credit desks, AVGO options traders, private-credit allocators, semis analysts.
</p>

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">U.S. July retail sales -0.6% M/M — biggest monthly drop in over a year, well below +0.1% est</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">U.S. July retail sales fell 0.6% M/M — the biggest monthly drop in more than a year and well below the +0.1% consensus.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
The Census Bureau's advance monthly retail trade report for July 2026, released Friday. Headline sales fell 0.6% from June to a seasonally adjusted $763.6B, missing the Dow Jones consensus of +0.1%. Year-over-year growth held at +5.0%.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Motor vehicles and parts led the drop at -1.8%, followed by nonstore retailers (online) at -2.2% and gasoline stations at -0.9%. Clothing (+1.9%), health and personal care (+0.7%), and food services (+0.5%) were the only gains. The advance estimate carries a ±0.4 pp sampling error.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The soft print landed the same morning as a miss on UMich prelim sentiment, pulling the S&P off Thursday's record high (closed -0.17% at 7,785.76). Coming after tame CPI and PPI, it sharpens focus on the Powell/Warsh Fed-chair transition and the September FOMC path.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Macro traders, rate desks, consumer sector analysts.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>

The capital-efficient approach is pitched as tailored for AI-infrastructure workloads driving hyperscaler NAND buys.

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">S&amp;P 500 +1.79% to record 7,736.52 · Dow first-ever close above 54,000</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">S&amp;P 500 sets first record close in two months and Dow tops 54,000 for the first time as tech rebounds and Hormuz talks brighten.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
U.S. stocks logged broad-based record closes on Tuesday Aug 4. The S&amp;P 500's 7,736.52 finish was its first record since early June, and the Dow's 54,085.88 was its first-ever settle above 54,000 — both indexes completing a four-session winning streak alongside the Nasdaq (+2.59% to 26,584.99) and Russell 2000.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Info Tech (+4.20%) and Industrials (+3.42%) led the tape, powered by Palantir +29.45% on 'otherworldly' Q2 results and Caterpillar +5.60% on its first-ever $20B+ quarter. WTI crude fell ~6% to $75.32/bbl as Treasury Sec. Bessent said a Strait of Hormuz deal could land 'today or tomorrow,' dragging the 10-year yield down 7bp to 4.61%.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The record closes reset market psychology after the July 29 FOMC hawkish hold. Tech's +4.20% session rebuilds the 'AI-capex earnings pay off' narrative just as SpaceX and AMD reported, and CAT's record backlog quantifies industrial-sector pull-through from the same data-center buildout.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Index traders, macro allocators, and AI-capex investors tracking the convergence of earnings beats and geopolitical easing.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Palantir posts $1.935B revenue on US commercial +149% Y/Y; raises FY26 guide to $8.15B; stock rockets to its best single session since Feb 2024.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Palantir reported Q2 2026 after Monday Aug 3's close with revenue of $1.935B (+93% Y/Y), beating the $1.81B estimate, and GAAP diluted EPS $0.41 topping $0.34 consensus. Shares gained 11.99% in after-hours before surging another +29.45% to $162.66 on the Aug 4 regular session.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
U.S. commercial revenue accelerated to $764M (+149% Y/Y) and U.S. government hit $809M (+90% Y/Y); adjusted operating margin was 62% and FCF margin 63%, driving Rule of 40 to 155. FY26 guidance was raised to $8.150–$8.158B (82% Y/Y), with U.S. commercial guided to at least $3.424B (+134% Y/Y minimum).
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The US commercial +149% acceleration is what bulls needed to confirm the AIP expansion, and the FY guide raise moves the conversation from valuation to growth durability. Palantir's 62% adj operating margin at nearly $2B quarterly revenue is elite for enterprise software and re-anchors AI-software expectations for NOW, CRM, and PATH.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
AI-software funds, growth-mandate PMs, and semis options traders positioning ahead of NVDA earnings Aug 26.

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Caterpillar (CAT) +5.60% on first-ever $20B+ quarter — adj EPS $8.17 vs $5.71 est, record $72.1B backlog</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Caterpillar posts its first-ever $20B+ quarter and record $72.1B backlog on AI-data-center power demand; FY26 sales guide raised to mid-to-high teens growth.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Caterpillar reported Q2 2026 before Tuesday's open with revenue of $20.5B (+24% Y/Y) — the first quarter in company history above $20B — and adj EPS $8.17, beating the $5.71 Street estimate by 43% and rising 73% Y/Y. GAAP EPS was $7.77 vs $4.62 a year ago.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
The backlog surged $9B sequentially to a record $72.1B (+92% Y/Y), paced by AI data-center power orders. Construction Industries led at $8.346B (+35%), followed by Power &amp; Energy $8.238B (+17%) and Resource Industries $4.648B (+20%); ME&amp;T free cash flow hit a record $5.1B. Management raised FY26 guidance to mid-to-high-teens sales growth.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
CAT's record backlog quantifies how AI-data-center capex is spilling into industrial power gear (large engines, generators, switchgear). The +5.60% session helped push the Dow to its first-ever close above 54,000, with clear read-throughs to Cummins (CMI), Eaton (ETN), Vertiv (VRT), and GE Vernova (GEV).
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Industrials analysts, AI-infra allocators, and dividend-growth investors tracking the data-center power supply chain.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">AMD posts a record quarter with data-center revenue up 107% and inks a 2GW MI450 Helios deal with Anthropic, but shares slide ~9% AH as guidance lands roughly in line with elevated expectations.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">

</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>

Despite the beat-and-raise, shares fell 8.81% to $472.89 in after-hours on 1.8× average volume, as the stock had already priced in a bigger blowout.

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The Anthropic 2GW MI450 pact is AMD's highest-profile hyperscaler win and puts a direct competitive pin in NVIDIA's Blackwell roadmap. The AH sell-off signals the market entered the print at stretched valuations; supply-chain read-throughs to AVGO, MRVL, and TSM ahead of NVDA's Aug 26 earnings.

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">SpaceX (SPCX) first-ever earnings: Q2 revenue $7.81B (+92% Y/Y), adj EBITDA $3.5B vs $2.0B est — but $18.4B capex sends SPCX -7% AH</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">SpaceX's debut public quarter beats on revenue and EBITDA — AI segment +247%, Starlink +66% — but $18.4B in capex, 40% above Street estimates, reignites the 'AI-capex return' worry.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
SpaceX reported its first-ever quarterly results as a public company (IPO'd Nasdaq under SPCX on June 12, 2026) after Tuesday's close. Q2 revenue of $7.81B (+92% Y/Y from $4.06B) beat the $6.93B consensus, and adj EBITDA of $3.5B nearly doubled the $2.0B estimate — all three reporting segments beat.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Connectivity (Starlink) delivered $4.291B (+66% Y/Y), AI segment $2.561B (+247%), and Space $962M (+29%). CFO Bret Johnsen guided to a $100B annualized run-rate by year-end and flagged an additional $6.7B in contracted cloud-services revenue starting October. Capital expenditures of $18.37B — roughly 40% above the ~$13B Street view — were the bear headline, sending shares -7% AH to $116.70.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The first earnings print re-anchors the SPCX story around AI compute and Starlink monetization rather than launch cadence, but the $18.4B capex line resets the timeline for when those investments earn a return. The sell-off mirrors the 'spend now, monetize later' playbook that has already pressured ORCL, MSFT, META, and GOOG.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
SPCX shareholders, AI-infra allocators, and space + telecom analysts benchmarking the Starlink monetization curve.
</p>

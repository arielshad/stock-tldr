<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Anthropic sets IPO price range at $70–$78 (ticker: ANT)</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Anthropic prices its Nasdaq IPO at $70–$78 — on track to become the biggest US listing ever.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Anthropic PBC, the Claude developer, filed an amended S-1/A with the SEC on Nov 10 setting the price range for its Nasdaq IPO under ticker ANT. The amendment covers 825M Class A shares at $70–$78, with a 123.75M-share greenshoe and a concurrent $26B private placement at IPO price.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
At the top of range, the deal raises up to $64.4B in gross proceeds — the largest US IPO ever, ahead of Saudi Aramco and Alibaba. Fully diluted valuation reaches up to $1.405T; pricing is set for Nov 18 with first trading Nov 19.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
This is the first time a pure-play frontier AI lab has set an IPO price range in a public filing. At $1T–$1.4T, Anthropic would list between Berkshire Hathaway and Alphabet on day one, anchoring private AI valuations at a pivotal moment in the AI capex narrative.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
IPO desks, AI-exposed allocators, and macro narrative traders watching the AI capex thesis get stress-tested in real time.
</p>

</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">CoreWeave's Q3 crushes top-line with 168% growth, backlog balloons to $132.5B, and FY26 guide is raised — shares jump ~16% overnight on Blue Ocean ATS.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>

</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Adjusted EBITDA was $2.256B at a 62% margin. Management raised FY26 revenue guidance to $13.0–$13.3B and set an exit-2026 annualized run-rate of $20–$21B, while FY26 capex guidance rose to $37–$39B. NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 systems came online during the quarter.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
After last week's $1T chip-stock rout, CoreWeave is the first big test of whether AI-infrastructure demand cracked. The answer: backlog exploded, guide went up, capex is still climbing — the opposite of a reset. Read-through is positive for NVDA, the neocloud cohort, and AI capex names.
</p>

<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
AI-infrastructure traders, semis desks, and high-growth large-cap investors tracking the AI capex cycle.

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Equities desk prints $6.44B (+72%) — Goldman stretches its lead in stock trading as IB fees jump 21% and ROE lands at 18.7%.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>

</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>

</p>

<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Trump Posts 'ENERGY CEASEFIRE' in Russia-Ukraine War; OFAC Waives Russian Diesel Sanctions Through Apr 2027</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Trump claims Russia-Ukraine "ENERGY CEASEFIRE" Sunday Oct 11, two days after OFAC waives Russian diesel sanctions through Apr 7, 2027 — Zelenskyy says it was "news for me."</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Following a Friday call with Putin, Treasury's OFAC issued General License 135 permitting all transactions in Russian-origin diesel through Apr 7, 2027. Trump then posted on Truth Social Sunday that Russia agreed to supply ~4.8M tons of diesel and declared an "ENERGY CEASEFIRE" — confirmed by neither Russia nor Ukraine.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
GL 135 is a temporary executive waiver; the 2022 Ending Importation of Russian Oil Act stays on the books, and UK/EU bans on Russian refined products remain. Zelenskyy told ABC he would halt strikes on Russian refineries if Russia halted attacks on Ukraine's energy grid — Russia made no such pledge and continued strikes through the weekend.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
First major US rollback of post-2022 Russia-oil sanctions, coming as US diesel averages $6.28/gal amid Hormuz tanker-attack supply strain. Analysts call the volumes "minuscule" vs ~2.94M bpd US daily diesel use; Brent barely moved in Asia pre-open ($103–105 range). A similar Trump energy-pause claim on Sep 14 did not hold.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Oil desks, refining-margin traders, and geopolitical/macro risk desks with exposure to Middle East and Russia-Ukraine energy flows.
</p>

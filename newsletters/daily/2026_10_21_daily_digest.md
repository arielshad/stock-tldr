<h2 style="margin: 0 0 8px; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 800; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.2;">Netflix (NFLX) Q3 2026 — Revenue $12.87B, EPS $0.87 beats; Q4 guide light, ad revenue cut to $2.7B; NFLX −5% AH</h2>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Netflix beats Q3 but Q4 revenue guide and a $300M ad-revenue cut push the stock ~5% lower in after-hours trade.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
Netflix reported Q3 2026 results after the Oct 20 close. Revenue grew 12% Y/Y to $12.87B, in line with Street expectations, while diluted EPS of $0.87 crushed the company's own $0.82 guide.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
The upside was almost entirely margin: operating income of $4.46B (+37% Y/Y) came from expense timing and tight cost control. Q4 guidance landed at $13.44B revenue (12% growth) and 26.0% op margin, after a ~120bp hit from recently announced headcount reductions.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
The ad-revenue cut matters more than the Q3 beat — $300M off the ad base is the second time this cycle Netflix has pushed ad-tech milestones to the right. The soft Q4 revenue line and lowered FCF guidance compound an already weak YTD setup.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Mega-cap tech &amp; streaming investors, ad-tech watchers (TTD, APP), and peers tracking the streaming ad ramp (DIS, WBD).
</p>

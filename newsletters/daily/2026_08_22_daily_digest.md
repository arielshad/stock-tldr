<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">US private-sector activity hit its fastest pace since April 2022 in August, all on the services side.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
S&amp;P Global's flash PMI for August came in at 56.0, up from 54.5 in July — the strongest reading in over four years. Services (56.8) beat the 54.0 consensus; manufacturing (53.2) missed at a 13-month low.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">How does it work?</strong><br>
Any reading above 50 signals expansion. Services ran at its fastest pace since December 2024 while manufacturing posted its weakest gain in 13 months on fading safety-stock build and lengthening supply delays.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Why does it matter?</strong><br>
S&amp;P Global's chief economist called it "the fastest output growth for over four years," with Q3 GDP now tracking ~3% annualized vs 1.5% in Q2. The strong print reduces near-term Fed easing odds — 10Y yields ticked higher on the release.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">Who is it for?</strong><br>
Macro traders, rates desks, and Fed watchers ahead of Chair Warsh's Jackson Hole speech on Aug 28.
</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px; font-size: 15px; color: #8a8a85; line-height: 1.5;">Trump's White House crypto summit and a Senate CLARITY Act push send Bitcoin +11.5% in a single session and light $3.3B of short positions on fire.</p>

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #f7ff00;">What is it?</strong><br>
President Trump convened crypto CEOs and the heads of the SEC and CFTC at the White House on Aug 19 and urged the Senate to pass the CLARITY Act. Bitcoin surged +11.5% to ~$71,834 — its biggest single-day gain since March and the first close above $70K since June.

<p style="margin: 0 0 16px; font-size: 15px; color: #f5f5f0; line-height: 1.6;">
